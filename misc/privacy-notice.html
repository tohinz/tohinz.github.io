Privacy Notice

Effective date: November 19, 2026
Last updated: November 19, 2026

This is the personal, non-commercial website of Tobias Hinz, California, USA. This notice explains what information is collected when you visit this website, how it is used, and what choices you have.

Summary

  • This website has no user accounts, sells nothing, and has no contact or sign-up forms.
  • I do not ask you for personal information such as your name or address.
  • Some information (for example your IP address and browser details) is collected automatically by the third-party services described below, which are needed to host and run the website or to measure how it is used.
  • I do not sell your personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Information Collected and How

When you visit this website, the following information may be collected automatically by the services listed in the next section:

  • Technical and request data: your IP address, the date and time of your visit, the page requested, the referring page, and your browser and operating system.
  • Usage statistics: pages visited, approximate location (derived from your IP address), and device and browser type, collected through Google Analytics using cookies.
  • Information you choose to provide: if you comment on a blog post via Disqus, Disqus processes the information you submit under its own privacy policy. If you e-mail me, I receive your e-mail address and whatever you include in your message.

This information is used to deliver and secure the website and to understand, in aggregate, how the website is used so that I can improve it.

Third-Party Services

This website uses the following third-party services, which receive information from your browser when you visit. Each service handles that information under its own privacy policy.

  • GitHub Pages (hosting): the website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security purposes. GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Cloudflare (DNS, CDN, proxy): requests to this website pass through Cloudflare, which provides DNS, content delivery, security, and performance optimization, and processes your IP address and request data to do so. Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics 4 (usage statistics): this website uses Google Analytics (measurement ID G-WY99S79WW0), which sets cookies to collect usage statistics such as pages visited, approximate location, and device and browser information. See How Google uses information from sites that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts: font files are loaded from Google's servers, which receive your IP address and browser information. Google Fonts privacy FAQ.
  • jsDelivr (MathJax): on some blog posts, MathJax is loaded from the jsDelivr content delivery network to render mathematical formulas. jsDelivr Privacy Policy.
  • Disqus (comments): blog posts include a Disqus comment widget. It loads when the comments section comes close to view (or immediately on browsers that do not support lazy loading). Once loaded, Disqus sets its own cookies and may track you. Disqus Privacy Policy.
  • Embedded content: some blog posts embed tweets from X/Twitter (loaded from platform.twitter.com) or GIFs from GIPHY. This content is loaded directly from those services, which may set cookies. X Privacy Policy, GIPHY Privacy Policy.

Cookies

The code of this website does not set any cookies of its own. Cookies are set by third-party services, in particular by Google Analytics and, when they load, by Disqus and by embedded X/Twitter or GIPHY content. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The website will still work, although comments and embedded content may not display.

Your Choices

  • Block or delete cookies using your browser settings.
  • Use a content or ad blocker to prevent third-party scripts from loading.
  • Install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on to stop Google Analytics from collecting data about your visits.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Some browsers can send a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal. This website does not change its behavior in response to DNT signals, and Google Analytics does not honor DNT. The website also does not specifically process Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals. If you do not want your visit measured, please use the browser controls, content blockers, or the Google Analytics opt-out add-on described above.

Sale and Sharing of Personal Information

I do not sell your personal information, and I do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

California Residents

Because this is a personal, non-commercial website, it does not meet the thresholds of the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). I nevertheless respect the privacy of California residents and will honor reasonable requests regarding your information, as described below.

Reviewing and Changing Your Information

I do not maintain user accounts or profiles. If you have questions about information related to you, or want to request that it be reviewed, corrected, or deleted (for example, an e-mail you sent me), contact me at tob [dot] hinz [at] gmail [dot] com. Information held by the third-party services listed above is managed by those services; please use the options described in their privacy policies.

Data Retention

Google Analytics data is kept according to Google Analytics' standard data retention settings. Data held by the other third-party services is retained according to their own policies. E-mails you send me are kept only as long as needed to respond.

Security

This website is served over HTTPS, which encrypts data in transit between your browser and the website. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, however, and I cannot guarantee absolute security.

International Data Transfers

The third-party services used by this website may process your information in the United States and in other countries, where data protection laws may differ from those where you live.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under the age of 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, in line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). If you believe a child has provided personal information through this website, please contact me and I will take steps to delete it.

Changes to This Notice

I may update this notice from time to time, for example when the services used by this website change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
